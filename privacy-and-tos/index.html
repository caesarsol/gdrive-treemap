Privacy Policy

Drive Treemap (“the App”), available at caesarsol.github.io/gdrive-treemap, shows how the storage space of your Google Drive is used, as a treemap. It is run by Cesare Soldini (“we”, “us”), the author of the App, as a free, non-commercial project. Its source code is public at github.com/caesarsol/gdrive-treemap, so everything described here can be checked against it.

In short

  • The App runs entirely in your browser. It has no server, no database and no user accounts of its own.
  • It reads the metadata of your Drive files (names, sizes, folders), never their contents.
  • That data goes straight from Google to your browser, is used only to draw the treemap, and is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking cookies.

Data the App accesses

When you choose Sign in with Google, Google asks you to grant the App one permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly (“See information about your Google Drive files”). With it, and only for the files you own, the App reads:

  • for each file and folder: its identifier, name, type (MIME type), size, the storage quota it uses, the folder it is in, whether it is in the trash and its link to open it in Drive;
  • for folders other people shared with you that hold files you own: their name, link and owner’s name, to show where those files are;
  • your account’s email address and display name, to show which account is signed in;
  • your storage quota (limit and usage), to show how full your account is.

The App cannot read, download, change, share or delete your files, and cannot see the files of other people.

How the data is used

The data is used only to draw the treemap, the per-category totals and the storage usage shown on the page, and to let you open a file in Google Drive when you click it. It is not used for any other purpose: not for advertising, not to build profiles, not to train machine-learning or AI models, and it is never sold.

Where the data goes and how long it is kept

  • In memory only. File metadata and the access token Google issues live in the browser tab’s memory. They are gone when you sign out, reload or close the tab. The access token expires after about one hour and is revoked when you choose Sign out.
  • Not on any server. Requests go directly from your browser to Google’s APIs. We operate no server that receives, stores or logs your Google data, and nothing is transferred to us or to third parties.
  • Browser storage. The App stores in your browser’s localStorage only your view preferences (“Show trashed files”, “Shallow”) and, if you typed one, an OAuth client ID. None of it is personal data; you can delete it by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Third parties

  • Google provides the sign-in (Google Identity Services) and the Google Drive API. Your use of them is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the App’s files. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when your browser downloads them; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not have access to these logs.
